# Getting Started

Follow these steps to set up your Oikion workspace.

## 1. Create Your Organization

After signing up, create your real estate agency organization — your workspace where all data lives.

## 2. Invite Your Team

Navigate to **Settings > Team** to invite agents and administrators.

## 3. Add Your First Property

Go to **MLS > New Property** and follow the wizard.

## 4. Set Up Your Public Profile

Visit **Settings > Agency Profile** and set visibility to **Public**.
